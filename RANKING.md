### Criteria

**1. Interval width.** Primary sort within a tier. Narrower means the historical spread is tighter.

**2. Tail risk as a rate.** Thresholding on the single worst observation flagged 5 of 6 schemes, because the worst value grows with sample size. The flag uses the share of evaluations losing more than 3pp, threshold 2<!-- claim: severe_rate_threshold_pct = 2.0000 -->%.

**3. Support.** `THIN_DATA` fires below 80<!-- claim: thin_rows_threshold = 80.0000 --> evaluations or 4<!-- claim: thin_families_threshold = 4.0000 --> model families.

## What this cannot do

- 0<!-- claim: intervals_excluding_zero = 0.0000 --> of 6 intervals exclude zero, so it cannot say a scheme will definitely cost accuracy.
- 19<!-- claim: n_big_losses_below_floor = 19.0000 --> of 25<!-- claim: n_big_losses = 25.0000 --> observed losses worse than 3pp fell below their interval floor.
- It is built on checkpoints Red Hat chose to publish, so damage from an untuned recipe is not represented.
- It reads 2 inputs. A 1B and a 70B model of the same scheme differ only through 6<!-- claim: n_size_dependent_widths = 6.0000 --> size-dependent widths.
