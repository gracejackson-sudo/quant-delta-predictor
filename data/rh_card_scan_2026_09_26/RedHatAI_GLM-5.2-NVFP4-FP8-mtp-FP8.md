This model contains FP8 MTP layers for use with `RedHatAI/GLM-5.2-NVFP4-FP8`

## Usage ##
```bash
hf download RedHatAI/GLM-5.2-NVFP4-FP8 --local-dir GLM-5.2-NVFP4-FP8
hf download RedHatAI/GLM-5.2-NVFP4-FP8-mtp-FP8 --local-dir GLM-5.2-NVFP4-FP8-mtp-FP8
cp GLM-5.2-NVFP4-FP8-mtp-FP8/* GLM-5.2-NVFP4-FP8
```
